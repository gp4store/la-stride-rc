Who We Are

A community
built on miles
and mornings.

LA Stride started with five friends, one beat-up speaker, and a Tuesday-night route through Echo Park. Six years later, we're still here — just bigger, faster, and a little bit louder.

"It's never about the pace. It's about who's beside you when the sun comes up over Griffith."

Every runner welcome. Every distance counts. Bring water, bring stories, leave with both.

Our Code

Stronger Together,
One Stride At a Time.

I.
SHOW UP

Rain, fog, marine layer, hangover. The streets don't care. We meet anyway, and that's half the work.

II.
RUN HONEST

No pace gatekeeping. No silent suffering. The fastest among us run the back. That's the rule.

III.
STAY AFTER

The run ends. The club doesn't. Coffee, breakfast, weird conversations on the curb — that's the whole point.

— FREE TO JOIN —

Lace up,
show up.

No membership fees, no qualifying times, no shoe brand requirements. Bring whatever you've got and meet us at the start.
